Display: Shippori Mincho B1 · Body: Noto Sans JP · Numbers: IBM Plex Mono

Motion in this direction

  • The red sun rises slowly behind the hero as you scroll.
  • A brush-stroke underline draws under the headline on load.
  • The ink line and N5/N4/N3 stamps fill in as you scroll the level-check section.
  • Sections fade up gently; cards lift slightly on hover.

Best for

A serious exam-prep brand that wants parents, employers and students to trust it at first glance.

次の駅は、N4。

Heavy Zen Kaku Gothic New for headings, Barlow Condensed for station numbers and signs, Noto Sans JP for reading.

Display: Zen Kaku Gothic New 900 · Signs: Barlow Condensed · Body: Noto Sans JP

Motion in this direction

  • Three skyline layers and the sun move at different speeds as you scroll (parallax).
  • The route line draws itself on scroll; stations light up and pulse when reached.
  • Each level has its own line colour, reused on badges, the dashboard and results.
  • In the real build, "How it works" becomes a pinned, scroll-driven scene on desktop.

Best for

Your brief: animation, parallax and a design people remember. The route metaphor explains the N5-first rule visually and follows the student from the level check to their results.

Trade-off: the most custom illustration and animation work. It needs discipline to keep the student area tidy.

日本語を、一歩ずつ着実に。

Zen Maru Gothic's rounded strokes feel friendly for beginners; Nunito matches it for Latin text and numbers.

Display & body: Zen Maru Gothic · Latin: Nunito

Motion in this direction

  • Four paper layers (sky, ridges, Mount Fuji, foreground hill with a torii) move at different depths as you scroll.
  • Clouds drift slowly across the sky.
  • Study cards lift and tilt like paper on hover.

Best for

Beginners at N5 and N4 who are nervous about exams. It feels welcoming and low-pressure.

Trade-off: can read as playful rather than exam-serious, and rounded type is a little less crisp for dense reading passages.

The same in every direction

  • Calm exam screen: no decorative motion, static timer digits, large answer targets, keyboard shortcuts (1–4 to answer, F to flag).
  • Everything editable in the CMS: headings, buttons, images, parallax layers, the animation used by each section, colours and the tagline, in English and Japanese.
  • Reduced motion: students who turn off motion on their device see a still version with every piece of content intact.
  • English first: every visitor starts in English, with a 日本語 switch in the navbar that is remembered.
  • Logo unchanged. Direction B uses a white-text version of the same logo on dark backgrounds.
